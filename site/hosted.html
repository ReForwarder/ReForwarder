The problem

Your email — someone else's rules.

Hosted email APIs price you by the message and keep the keys to your reputation. Three things every growing team runs into:

01

Every send is a line item.

Password resets, receipts, campaigns — each one bills. The better your product does, the bigger the invoice gets.

02

Your reputation lives on shared infrastructure.

You warm IPs you'll never own and share pools with strangers. One bad neighbor, and your deliverability pays for it.

03

Your audience data is a hostage.

Subscribers, engagement history, suppression lists — exported as CSVs on request, stored on someone else's terms.

The alternative

Bring the platform in-house.

ReForwarder is the whole stack — the API, the pipeline, the database — running on hardware you own, behind domains you control.

Zero to first send

Running it takes minutes, not migrations.

No sales call, no provisioning wait. The stack starts on your machine and the dashboard comes up with it.

STEP 01

Start the stack

One Docker Compose command brings up the API, dashboard, worker, PostgreSQL, and Redis. The dashboard answers on port 9000.

$ docker compose up -d
STEP 02

Verify a domain

Add your sending domain and publish the SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records the dashboard shows you. Mail from your domain, authenticated by your records.

STEP 03

Attach an SMTP route

Connect any SMTP server — your own MTA or a provider you already trust — and delivery flows through your pipeline, under your throttles.

STEP 04

Send your first email

Create an API Key and make one call to /api/v1/emails/send. Watch it land in Emails with full event history.

FAQ

Fair questions.

Is ReForwarder really free?

The self-hosted edition is — AGPL-3.0-or-later, no paid tiers or feature gates. You cover your own infrastructure. If you modify it and offer it as a network service, the license asks you to share your modified source.

Will there be a hosted version? Is it paid?

Yes. A fully managed ReForwarder cloud is coming, and it will be a paid service — we operate the servers, upgrades and backups so you don't have to. Self-hosting stays free. See the hosted page for details.

What do I need to run it?

A machine with Docker Compose, a domain you control, and an SMTP route for delivery — your own mail server or any provider. PostgreSQL and Redis ship in the Compose file, and the dashboard lives at localhost:9000 out of the box.

How is this different from SendGrid or Mailgun?

They're hosted services that bill per message and keep your data and reputation on their infrastructure. ReForwarder is the same kind of platform running on your hardware — one HTTP API instead of a dashboard you rent.

Can I migrate from my current provider gradually?

Yes. Point existing SMTP clients at the built-in relay — no code changes — and move API calls over endpoint by endpoint. Your domains just need their DNS records pointed at your setup.

Where does my data actually live?

In your PostgreSQL, on your disks. API keys are stored hashed, accounts support two-factor authentication, workspaces enforce role-based access, and every administrative action lands in the audit log. Email content never leaves your infrastructure — the only outside call is a version update check.
